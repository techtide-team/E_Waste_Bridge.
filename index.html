WHY E-WASTE BRIDGE?

Bridging the gap between waste and opportunity.

Electronic devices are discarded every day. Yet many contain components that can be repaired, refurbished, reused or responsibly recycled.

E-Waste Bridge creates a digital connection between people and institutions with electronic waste and organizations equipped to process it.
